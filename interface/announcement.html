Drainage Clearing Activity

📢 LGU Odiongan, Romblon Announcement The Local Government Unit (LGU) of Odiongan, Romblon will conduct a Drainage Clearing Activity to remove leaves, litter, and other debris that obstruct drainage systems. This initiative aims to improve water flow, reduce the risk of flooding, and promote a cleaner and healthier environment for everyone. 🗓 Date: August 20, 2026 📍Location: Selected roads and drainage areas throughout the Municipality of Odiongan. We encourage all residents, barangay officials, volunteers, and community organizations to participate and support this activity. Together, we can help maintain a safe, clean, and flood-resilient community. "Clean Drainage, Safer Community.
